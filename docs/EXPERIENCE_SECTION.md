# What Not to Do

Avoid:

* copying the resume bullet-for-bullet
* giant job cards
* skill percentage bars
* company logo wall
* fake promotion titles
* exaggerated experience years
* describing every task ever completed
* full case-study content inside each role
* employment statistics without evidence

This section provides **career context**, not every implementation detail.
