# CONTACT_SECTION.md

## Purpose

This is the final major section before the footer.

Its job is to convert interest into action.

By this point the visitor has already seen:

```text
Hero
→ positioning

What I Build
→ engineering focus

Rasterex
→ production proof

PlayLight
→ zero-to-one ownership

Experience
→ career progression

Engineering Thinking
→ technical depth

About
→ engineering philosophy
```

The Contact section should now answer:

> Interested? What is the easiest way to talk to Naveed?

Do not introduce new proof here.

---

# Section Eyebrow

Recommended:

```text
LET'S TALK
```

Alternative:

```text
CONTACT
```

Prefer:

**LET'S TALK**

It feels slightly more human without becoming sales-oriented.

---

# Main Heading

Recommended:

# Looking for a backend engineer who can own more than just endpoints?

Alternative:

# Building something where backend reliability and product thinking matter?

Preferred:

**Looking for a backend engineer who can own more than just endpoints?**

This connects directly to the story established throughout the site.

---

# Supporting Copy

Recommended:

> I’m open to backend, product engineering, developer-platform, integration, and selected AI-backend opportunities. If you’re building a serious product and think my experience could be useful, I’d be glad to talk.

Keep this short.

Do not repeat the entire resume.

# Closing Motion

The site has had movement throughout.

Here, motion should become quiet.

Possible sequence:

```text
background nodes slow down
↓

↓
one final node activates
↓

```

The feeling should be:

**the system has reached its destination.**

---

# CTA Motion

Keep simple.

On hover:

```text
Email me →
          →
```

The arrow may move a few pixels.

The button or link can gain slightly stronger contrast.

No glow.

No large scale animation.

---

# Contact Form

A contact form is optional.

My recommendation for the first version:

**do not add one unless there is a clear reason.**

Direct email is:

* simpler
* more reliable
* less infrastructure
* easier to maintain
* less spam/security surface

If a contact form is introduced later, follow `AGENTS.md` for:

* server-side validation
* abuse protection
* error handling
* provider secrets
* duplicate submission handling

# Tone

Use:

* open
* confident
* professional
* direct

Avoid:

```text
Let's create something amazing together!
Have a crazy idea?
Let's build the future!
Hire me today!
Let's turn your vision into reality!
```

This portfolio is for serious engineering opportunities.

---

# Location

Optionally include:

```text
Islamabad, Pakistan
```

with:

```text
Remote worldwide
```

or:

```text
Open to remote and international opportunities
```

No need for a map.

---

# Response Expectations

Do not make promises like:

```text
I reply within 24 hours.
```

unless that is something you genuinely want to guarantee.

Keep contact expectations simple.

---

# Accessibility

Ensure:

* email is a real `mailto:` link
* links have visible focus states
* icons have accessible labels
* CTA works without animation
* external links are clearly indicated
* text remains selectable

---

# Mobile

Mobile layout should be simple:

```text
LET'S TALK

Heading

Supporting copy

Email me →

email address

LinkedIn
GitHub
Resume
```

No complex sticky behavior.

Large touch targets.

---

# What Not to Include

Avoid:

* complicated contact forms
* phone number as primary CTA
* WhatsApp button unless intentionally required
* calendar booking widget by default
* freelance pricing
* "services"
* hourly rates
* giant social icon row
* animated envelope illustrations
* new technical claims
* unnecessary statistics

This section is about **contact**, not more persuasion.
